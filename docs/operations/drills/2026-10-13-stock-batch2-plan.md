## Target and evidence

AH-SEC-02 is Critical: authenticated billing staff can fabricate stock and change selling price outside the trusted price endpoint, bypassing required MRP/ceiling/reason, last-editor attribution and owner review. Original isolated action: batch a manual-inward movement from quantity 20 to 120/revision 1 to 2, and update the product to quantity 120/selling price 100 paise. Observed: accepted, instead of unchanged quantity 20/selling price 9000; no stockPriceChanges record or lastPriceEdit. [Original finding](2026-10-12-drill-01.md#ah-sec-02---forged-movement-bypasses-stock-and-price-safeguards), [preserved evidence](../../../output/playwright/2026-10-12-drill-01/batches/financial-1791816533104/financial-SEC-03-fabricate-19898-ice-bypass-must-be-rejected/evidence.json).

Root cause: the generic movement/product Rules branch validates arithmetic and a client-supplied linked movement, not an independently authorized business operation. Product updates through that branch include sourceValues (MRP) and sellingPricePaise. Owner create/delete, metadata, cost, receipt/audit and event branches also need inventory; adding an explicit deny alongside a matching allow does not close a bypass. Admin SDK bypasses Rules, so replacement handlers must enforce authorization and source validity themselves.

Planning work inspected current repositories, mounted call sites, Rules and server handlers. No fresh attack, browser mutation or Batch 2 scenario was executed. Source-level risks below are distinguished from the original confirmed drill failure. No additional Batch 1 failure was observed during this read-only preparation.

Reject anonymous, missing, disabled or rejected profiles. Use existing region asia-south1 and Node20; no dependencies/runtime/service upgrades. Existing read permissions remain unchanged, including owner-only price review and owner-only Audit History UI.

Proposed callable contracts (names subject to implementation convention):

1. **createStockProduct**: operationId, normalized barcode, name and selling-price intent for zero-stock POS creation; no caller quantity/revision/actor. Return existing matching active identity or create one product/event atomically. GRN creates its new product inside receipt posting, not a prior standalone commit.
2. **saveStockReceiptDraft / deleteStockReceiptDraft**: source ID, operationId, expected source revision and draft intent. Save is not receipt posting. Preserve invalid/incomplete draft editing where supported while fully validating submission. Recover a committed delete through an operation receipt rather than recreating a deleted draft.
3. **submitStockReceipt**: receipt ID, operationId, expected draft revision and existing product revision/price snapshots from scan or explicit refresh confirmation. Read stored draft, products/costs, vendor, V2 activation/config, invoice reservation/purchase/state/account/original GRN. Require real active vendor, positive whole quantities, positive integer costs, required GRN MRP and selling <= MRP, valid business dates and distinct lines. Derive line stock value and quantities. Full invoice payable is separately entered and must NOT equal the received-line sum for partial receipts. Normalize invoice numbers using the existing NFKC/whitespace/case contract. One invoice creates one payable; later matching partial receipts link it without a second payable. Reject incompatible vendor/total/source or stale revision atomically; no partial stock-only success.
4. **reverseStockReceipt**: receipt ID, operationId, expected receipt revision and reason, plus a confirmed preview of relevant current product/cost/account/invoice revisions. Recompute reversal from canonical original/partial receipt, original purchase and current outstanding/reservations. Reject linked partials still present on original reversal and reserved funds. Keep later unrelated costs/prices. Negative stock remains permitted. Replaying an original reversal returns its original result, not another quantity deduction/credit.
5. **beginStockAudit / postStockAuditGroup / finishStockAudit**: actor-bound batch ID, ordered product/count/revision intents, reason and optional missing-cost values; stable group IDs and intent hashes. Server reads canonical products/costs and records matched counts without stock movement/revision increment; adjusted counts derive delta/value and advance revision. Recorded cost wins; staff may fill only a genuinely unknown cost, including zero where current audit policy permits. Finish derives totals from committed group receipts; it cannot accept caller totals or claim an unposted group succeeded. Explicit interrupted completion preserves posted groups. Known stale rows require refreshed confirmation, not silent retry against a newer count.
6. Existing **changeStockPrices / reviewStockPriceChange**: same required MRP, zero/equal valid prices, ceiling, reason, stale check and replay. Add new-write source/provenance evidence only. Review stays acknowledgement and must never update products.
7. **mapLegacyStockLine**, retained metadata/manual-cost operations if needed: narrow source-specific contracts above, not arbitrary Admin-SDK document patching. Disable unused browser posting exports even if replacements have no mounted UI. Before implementing retained legacy endpoints, verify their downstream return behavior; an unresolved legacy source is a blocked compatibility test, not permission to guess.

Operation identity must survive ambiguous acknowledgement and refresh. Reuse source IDs where possible; otherwise add a small server-only operation receipt containing actor, operation type, normalized intent hash and immutable result. This is recovery metadata, not another stock ledger. Same actor/ID/intent returns the original result before current stale checks; different actor or changed payload rejects. Read records before writing; use deterministic linked evidence IDs, integer paise, safe integer checks and server timestamps. Caller date is validated business intent for receipts; audit uses server IST business date, never timestamp.slice(0,10) UTC. Fixing that audit-date calculation is an intrinsic part of replacing its writer; no unrelated timezone rewrite.

Barcode control proposal: additive server-only posSandboxes/test/barcodeReservations keyed by normalized barcode hash, reserving exact and valid UPC/EAN aliases together. Resolve existing catalogue identities lazily, without catalogue migration/backfill. Legacy duplicates or inactive matches must return an explicit conflict; never silently merge/reactivate. Race-test POS creation against GRN/new product creation and any retained metadata barcode change. Verify transaction-query/uniqueness semantics in the installed SDK before finalizing this implementation; a reservation must not create a second product when a pre-existing legacy ID exists. Internal operation/group/control documents are not new public data paths; deny their client writes/reads except a deliberately minimal recovery result where required.

## Price review and reversal policy proposed for approval

Preserve existing intake editing, including MRP on Receive Stock; the removed retrospective GRN MRP-correction button remains removed. Proposed protection: every effective price change to an existing product made by receiving or reversal generates a pending stockPriceChanges record atomically, with sourceKind/sourceRecordId, reason/context, real before/after, revisions and lastPriceEdit. Use its existing receipt/reversal stock movement as the history row; add price evidence there instead of a second zero-quantity movement. Current Stock corrections retain their existing price movement. New-product initial prices are creation evidence, not a fictitious correction from zero. Stock History event-type filtering must intentionally match price-bearing receipt/reversal rows once, including when multiple types are selected.

New GRN and manual-price writes track separate MRP and selling-price provenance, including prior field provenance on receipt lines. Reversal restores a field only when this receipt still owns its current value. An intervening edit that changes a value and later changes it back remains a later edit; equality alone cannot authorize restoration. If a proposed restoration would make selling > MRP, retain the current valid pair and clearly report the skipped restoration rather than post an invalid pair. Owner sees the exact effect in reversal confirmation before committing.

Legacy receipts lack reliable field provenance. Proposed conservative behavior: reverse their recorded stock/payable normally, but preserve current prices when ownership is ambiguous and show “Price restoration unavailable: historical provenance not recorded.” Keep known historical before values visible; do not invent ownership, write backfills or silently replay them. Owner can use Current Stock Edit to correct prices afterward. This intentionally narrows automatic legacy price restoration and needs approval as a compatibility policy. Cost reversal retains current behavior: invalidate only a cost still tagged to this GRN; do not guess/restore an earlier unrecorded cost.

## Rules cutover and the refund dependency

**There is no safe single stock-only cutover that preserves the current browser refund transaction.** approvePosRequest writes product restitution and financial/counter/state effects together. A separate stock callable before/after that transaction would break atomicity; keeping the generic client movement branch leaves AH-SEC-02 open. No new client posting permit or unsafe fallback is proposed.

Recommended staged approval boundary:

1. **B2-A, replacements and price protection:** implement trusted mounted stock writers/recovery and narrow adapters; remove direct price/sourceValues/product-create/cost/receipt/audit posting permissions once their corresponding legitimate replacement exists. For any temporary quantity-only compatibility path required by the existing refund writer, document its exact scope and attack results. Never add a new broader allow, claim stock authority complete, or deploy this intermediate state. Prefer holding the final product/movement Rule fence as an explicit staged patch rather than pretending it passes all workflows.
2. **B2-B, final authority fence:** after separately approved trusted atomic return/void implementation, deny ALL client product and movement business writes, cost writes, posted receipt/audit/batch writes and deletes. The retained narrow metadata/create/cost/mapping operations must already be trusted or disabled. Existing source/detail reads stay compatible. Re-run the original forgery and legitimate refunds together. Only this milestone can close AH-SEC-02.

B2-A price protection cannot be achieved merely by excluding sellingPricePaise: sourceValues embeds MRP and can carry other pricing/valuation evidence. Deny client price-bearing fields across every overlapping allow, including creation and legacy owner branches; server derives metadata. Do not leave fake price movements that make History look trusted: trusted price records and corresponding events/movements are server-only, even at the staged boundary. Residual client quantity forgery until B2-B is a known Critical risk, not accepted mitigation.
