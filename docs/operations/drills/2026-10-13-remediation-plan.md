## Confirmed application bugs

### AH-SEC-03 — checkout accepts supplied prices and actor role

- **Severity/business impact:** Critical, P0. Authenticated billing staff can undercharge and bypass their discount cap. A catalogue item priced at 9,000 paise was sold for 1 paise. A billing UID supplying owner role received a 99% discount and stored that false role.
- **Root cause:** [posRepository.ts](../../../src/features/pos/data/posRepository.ts#L819) computes totals from caller lines and passes caller actor to discount validation. Current product reads check revision, not equality to canonical price. Rules do not independently establish canonical prices or the real profile role. See [posDomain.ts](../../../src/features/pos/domain/posDomain.ts#L58) and [firestore.rules](../../../firestore.rules#L1635).
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** Add a callable checkout handler under functions, wired through [functions/index.js](../../../functions/index.js). Read authenticated profile, current products, checkout configuration and counter state in the trusted transaction; recompute money in integer paise. Submit product IDs, quantities, expected revisions, discount intent, payments and a stable operation ID. Remove authority from supplied actor/price/totals. Adapt posRepository, [POS checkout UI](../../../src/features/pos/components/PosPage.tsx#L219), POS types/errors and targeted Rules. Preserve the existing manager/owner discount policy; do not add an arbitrary-price override by implication.
- **History/compatibility:** Keep existing bill fields, receipt format, sequence, financial-year conventions, paths and immutable historical snapshots. Older bills keep their recorded prices. Zero/equal prices and currently supported negative-stock sales retain their existing policy. Stale scanned prices must produce a refresh/review result, not silently charge a new price. All active staff retain the approved Current Stock price-edit permission.
- **Regression:** Original forged-price and forged-role tests; owner/manager/billing limits, disabled/missing/anonymous profiles, changed configuration, stale products, zero-price goods, unknown items, duplicate product lines, split payments, change, holds and receipt printing. Direct callable requests must be tested separately from button visibility.
- **Acceptance:** Forged price/role attempts produce no bill, stock, sequence, state, event or counter change. Genuine A×2 + B at 9,000/4,500 paise gives subtotal 22,500; 5% discount 1,125; total 21,375. Authenticated billing cannot receive owner privileges by payload. Persisted actor role/UID comes from trusted identity.

### AH-SEC-04 — bill creation can omit stock deduction

- **Severity/business impact:** Critical, P0. Sales/cash and physical inventory diverge while a bill looks valid. Two 18,000-paise bills were accepted but stock stayed at 18 rather than 16, starting from 20 units.
- **Root cause:** [bill Rules](../../../firestore.rules#L1635) check sequence, state and selected counter links without proving all product deductions. A source-shaped direct batch passes outside a count reservation. The same control was denied during a live reservation: the count-lock check is working and must be retained.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** In the same trusted checkout transaction as AH-SEC-03, create the bill, state, sequence, aggregated per-product sale movements, product revisions, event, cashier state and counter totals. Deny independent client bill creation and sale-side companion writes after replacement is ready. Inspect shared handover branches before changing them: refund, count, join, cancellation and closure must remain usable. Files include posRepository, functions checkout handler/index, [cashierHandoverRepository.ts](../../../src/features/pos/data/cashierHandoverRepository.ts), [cashierHandover.ts](../../../src/features/pos/domain/cashierHandover.ts), types and Rules.
- **History/compatibility:** No backfill or deletion of existing bills/movements; retain source IDs for Stock History and Action Centre. Existing GRN/audit/return writers cannot be blanket-denied as part of a checkout-only change. Old installed clients must get an explicit reload-required failure instead of retaining an unsafe direct-write fallback.
- **Regression:** Original omitted-stock batch with and without active counts; concurrent terminals; same-intent replay; stale revision; payment mismatch; failed transaction; duplicate SKU lines; current 200-line limit; counter open/join/auth-time membership; reservation cancel/expiry; logout and Cashout effects; held-cart resume. Verify all source records independently after each write.
- **Acceptance:** Each accepted checkout has exactly one bill/state/sequence advance and the corresponding stock/movement/cash effects. A replay returns the same bill without new effects. Count-prohibited or rejected attempts leave every affected record unchanged. Two valid A×2 bills result in stock 16 and cash 36,000 paise, with unique receipt sequences.

### AH-SEC-02 — fabricated movement changes stock and bypasses price review

- **Severity/business impact:** Critical, P0. Billing staff can invent 100 units and reduce a selling price from 9,000 to 100 paise without GRN/count evidence or owner price-change review.
- **Root cause:** [validProductStockMovement](../../../firestore.rules#L1198) proves the product and movement agree with each other, not that their business source is genuine. The [product update branch](../../../firestore.rules#L1488) includes sourceValues and sellingPricePaise; [movement creation](../../../firestore.rules#L1535) accepts generic arithmetic with no required authorized posting source.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** Replace generic stock posting with source-specific trusted operations for physical counts, receiving and reversal, reusing existing posting logic/contracts. Inventory all product writers, including unknown-item creation, temporary mapping and import tooling, before narrowing permissions. Catalogue price edits use the existing price callable; legitimate GRN pricing/restoration requires trusted receipt validation. Distinguish metadata edits from quantities, costs and prices. A narrow field allowlist can reduce the price bypass earlier, but it cannot be declared a complete fix for fabricated quantity/source records. Files: Rules, posRepository, existing functions/stockPrices.js, new stock posting handlers, GRN/audit repositories/UI adapters and tests.
- **History/compatibility:** Keep current product, cost, GRN, audit, vendor payable and movement paths. Retain all-staff approved price editing and owner review, which must not reapply old prices. Do not deny all product updates while browser-owned receiving/audit still needs them. Preserve owner GRN reversal and vendor invoice links. No production catalogue cleanup is included.
- **Regression:** Original generic movement/price batch; nonexistent/wrong/reused source IDs; false actor; stale revision; double submit; simultaneous sale/count/receipt/reversal; new versus existing product; MRP ceiling/zero/equal price; legitimate price review; downstream sale/price/payment reversal variants and vendor payable invariants.
- **Acceptance:** Forged batch leaves quantity 20, selling price 9,000, revision and review evidence unchanged. Every quantity/cost/price change has a permitted trusted source and exactly-once evidence. A physical count of 17 records delta −3; GRN A×3 at cost 6,000 increases stock by 3 and payable by 18,000 paise. Quantity edits continue through Stock Audit.

### AH-SEC-01 — staff can create an orphan refund event

- **Severity/business impact:** Critical, P0. Forged financial history can mislead refund/net-sales/history consumers. The accepted event claimed a 500,000-paise refund without a bill or approval. Effects on every downstream dashboard were not measured.
- **Root cause:** [events Rules](../../../firestore.rules#L1689) authorize staff/actor identity without proving an approved source or a genuine posting.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** Generate return/void events only within the trusted owner decision transaction described in AH-FIN-01. Bind source bill, approval ID/revision, quantities, condition, money, actor and date. Deny independent client financial-event creation by type once all writers for those types are replaced; preserve other legitimate event types until audited. Change functions, posRepository approve/reject adapters, Action Centre source/decision integration where needed, and Rules.
- **History/compatibility:** Existing historical events remain readable, including legacy records lacking fields. No automatic deletion, invented links or totals rewrite. Retain bill-level damaged returns with no stock change and the approved product-filter limitation.
- **Regression:** Original orphan write; non-owner decision; invented approval; stale/double approval; matching source with false money/actor; rejection/withdrawal behavior where supported; damaged and sellable returns; unified history de-duplication, source opening and pagination.
- **Acceptance:** Orphan/fabricated events are rejected with no source or ledger changes. One genuine decision produces one source-linked event; replay cannot create another. Owner/staff permissions remain enforced on direct requests.

### AH-FIN-01 — one returned unit can refund the entire bill

- **Latest workflow decisions, superseding earlier approval assumptions:** All active staff roles complete returns/refunds immediately through a trusted server operation; owner approval is not required. Owner gets a completed-return Action Centre notification and acknowledgement cannot reapply stock/money. Completely retire new void functionality in Batch3 while preserving historical void reads. Add read-only View bill popup/eye action. The [revised Batch3 proposal](2026-10-14-refund-batch3-plan.md) contains current roles, posting/review separation, linked expense/cash accounting and explicit replacements for obsolete positive void tests. Earlier owner-decision descriptions below remain original discovery/proposal context, not the current required UX.
- **Subsequent cash workflow requirement:** New customer returns always pay Cash, regardless of original sale channel; the refund amount populates from the selected remaining quantities. Approval atomically creates one protected linked POS Refund cash expense and updates drawer/stock/source effects. Pending/rejected requests create no expense. See the [complete Batch3 proposal](2026-10-14-refund-batch3-plan.md) for double-deduction prevention, operating-expense separation, prospective dates/counter rules, void distinction and required consumer regression. This supersedes the prior new-return method-selection proposal; historical methods remain readable. Implementation approval is pending.
- **User-confirmed return requirements, 14 October 2026:** A bill line's combined sellable/damaged return quantity cannot exceed its originally billed quantity minus prior approved returns; fully returned lines have no remaining return allowance. Show billed, already returned and remaining quantities in the return UI. Automatically populate and update the refund quote whenever selected quantities change, using original bill prices after allocated discounts. Empty selection produces zero, never a whole-bill default. The server independently checks remaining quantity and refundable money again on posting/approval, including concurrent requests. These requirements are inputs to the separate Batch3 proposal, not implementation or deployment authorization. Whether a deliberately smaller manual refund remains available must be explicit in that proposal; auto-population alone does not approve a new override policy.
- **Additional regression/acceptance:** A line billed as2 units at9000 paise each initially allows at most2 units and quotes9000 for1 /18000 for2. After one approved return of1 unit/refund9000, it allows at most1 more and quotes9000; after both returns it allows0. Combined sellable/damaged selections cannot reuse the same billed units. Increasing/decreasing/clearing selections updates the quote; discounted mixed-line and paise-rounding cases reconcile independently. Over-quantity and stale/concurrent approvals reject without partial effects. No historical bill/refund rewrite.
- **Severity/business impact:** High, P0. Confirmed over-refund/cash leakage: A×2 sold for 18,000 paise; returning one unit refunded 18,000 rather than at most 9,000 and restored only one unit.
- **Root cause:** [ReturnDialog](../../../src/features/pos/components/PosPage.tsx#L458) defaults a blank refund to total bill value. [approvePosRequest](../../../src/features/pos/data/posRepository.ts#L1030) validates cumulative quantity but trusts requested refund value/method, without a line-net or remaining-money cap.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** Display a selected-quantity refund quote; compute the authoritative maximum on the server using immutable bill prices, allocated original discounts, prior returns and remaining refundable money. Revalidate on owner approval, not just request entry. Make approval/state/refund ledger/restoration/event posting atomic and idempotent; preserve existing void and pre-handover handling. Define deterministic discount rounding and legacy partial-refund treatment before implementation. Goodwill refunds above sale value are a separate, explicitly authorized requirement, not an implicit bypass. Files: PosPage ReturnDialog, posRepository, POS domain/types, trusted decision handlers, Rules and Action Centre adapters.
- **History/compatibility:** Never calculate a return from today's catalogue price. Preserve historical bills and completed refunds; flag ambiguous legacy evidence rather than guessing allocations or backfilling money. Proposed default is pro-rata allocation of bill discount with deterministic paise remainder handling; seek a policy decision if a different refund model is intended.
- **Regression:** Original over-refund; zero/full/partial/successive returns; percentage/fixed discount rounding; duplicate/foreign line IDs; over-quantity; sellable/damaged; cash/noncash/split original payments; concurrent decisions; stale request; owner reject; void-after-return; count locks and legacy bills.
- **Acceptance:** The 18,000-paise one-unit request is rejected and leaves stock 18/cash 18,000 unchanged. A valid 9,000-paise request restores stock to 19 and leaves cash 9,000. Cumulative refunds cannot exceed remaining eligible net value. A stale/replayed decision cannot post twice. Every allowed refund has traceable quantity and price evidence.

### AH-SEC-05 — negative expenses and forged approval text are accepted

- **Severity/business impact:** High, P1. Staff can write a negative ₹2,500 expense and owner-labelled approval text, potentially corrupting expense summaries and accountability. Production damage and every derived total were not assessed.
- **Root cause:** [cashouts Rules](../../../firestore.rules#L926) allow staff creates without monetary/schema/approval validation. UI minimums do not protect direct SDK writes.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** A trusted expense-create operation validates authenticated profile, permitted fields/category/payment/cheque data, date and nonnegative money at two-decimal precision using integer paise internally. Bind creator UID/name; derive approval status under the agreed policy and reject forged approval fields. Deny client creates after replacement; retain separately authorized owner correction/delete behavior until reviewed. Files: [ExpenseForm](../../../src/features/register/components/ExpenseForm.tsx), [createFinanceActions](../../../src/store/actions/createFinanceActions.ts), finance types, functions and Rules. Resolve AH-GAP-02 before adding an approval queue; validating values and rejecting false approval does not require inventing that queue.
- **History/compatibility:** Existing cashouts.amount is rupees, not paise. Preserve that external schema or approve an explicit additive adapter; never reinterpret existing values as paise. Zero is currently allowed by the UI; retain nonnegative policy unless owner chooses strictly positive. Do not normalize, delete or change old expense approval labels.
- **Regression:** Negative/nonfinite/overprecision/string values; zero and ordinary expense; false owner label; all roles; category/payment restrictions; cheque confirm/cancel; replay; owner correction and linked Logs/totals.
- **Acceptance:** The negative/forged payload creates no document or financial side effects. Valid ₹25 creates one record equivalent to 2,500 paise with authenticated creator identity. Billing cannot claim an owner's decision. Existing expense views retain compatible amounts.

### AH-FIN-03 — Cash Movement double-click posts two transfers

- **Severity/business impact:** High, P1. One intended ₹300 movement from ₹1,000 posted twice, debiting 60,000 paise and leaving ₹400 instead of ₹700. Reproduced twice through the real UI.
- **Root cause:** [CashMovementForm](../../../src/features/cash-movement/components/CashMovementForm.tsx#L84) has no immediate submit guard. [saveCashTransfer](../../../src/store/actions/createFinanceActions.ts#L565) generates a new operation ID on each invocation. The existing callable's same-ID idempotency and concurrent overspend protection passed; the UI created two distinct intents.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** Acquire an immediate in-flight ref before awaiting, disable submit and show pending state. Generate one operation ID per user intent and retain it through recoverable failure, refresh recovery and lost acknowledgement until definitive completion/cancellation. Pass that ID through store/action types. Review the callable's existing-ID branch for actor/payload binding and original-result recovery; mismatched-ID replay is investigation needed, not a separately confirmed exploit. Files: CashMovementForm, createFinanceActions, relevant action/app types, functions/index.js and tests.
- **History/compatibility:** Keep existing transfer IDs/records/balance calculation. Do not delete presumed duplicate historical transfers; any reconciliation requires a separate reviewed correction. A new deliberate transfer gets a new ID; retrying a completed intent does not.
- **Regression:** Original double-click, rapid Enter, slow response, concurrent tabs, commit followed by lost response/reconnect/refresh, same-ID changed payload, insufficient balance and two independent valid intents. Assert both sender and recipient canonical balances.
- **Acceptance:** One ₹300 intent creates one transfer, debit 30,000 paise, sender ₹700 and recipient ₹300. Replaying it has no new effect. Two deliberate intents remain possible. An ambiguous network outcome never generates a replacement ID automatically.

### AH-FIN-02 — matched shared drawer is labelled excess cash

- **Severity/business impact:** Medium, P1. False discrepancy reporting can trigger incorrect staff accountability/corrections. Closure correctly recorded expected=counted=100,000 paise, difference 0; Daily Cashout recorded cash-more and −₹1,000.
- **Root cause:** [DailyCashoutForm](../../../src/features/cashout/components/DailyCashoutForm.tsx#L98) compares physical cash to today's cash sales; [saveDailyCashoutEntry](../../../src/store/actions/createFinanceActions.ts#L385) repeats that basis. [drawerClosure](../../../src/features/pos/domain/drawerClosure.ts) uses checkpoint plus ledger changes. Two meanings of audit are conflated.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** Use the canonical drawer closure expected/count/difference for physical discrepancy UI and new Daily Cashout audit fields. Keep sales metrics separate. Establish whether each expense/vendor payment is funded from this drawer before adding it as an outflow; that attribution is investigation/requirement work, not an inferred arithmetic fix. Update form, finance action, cashoutCorrections/domain types and Logs/Action Centre discrepancy displays as needed. Preserve closure/correction authorization and holder balances.
- **History/compatibility:** Paise closure versus rupee legacy audit fields require explicit conversion and a documented difference sign. Add an audit-basis/schema marker for new records if needed so old sales-based fields are not silently reinterpreted. No historical totals or closure rewrite; legacy source detail should explain the recorded basis.
- **Regression:** Opening/checkpoint carryover, cash and noncash sales, refunds, denomination totals, genuine shortage/excess, repeated close, correction approve/reject, holder transfers, zero sales and mixed expenses after funding policy is decided.
- **Acceptance:** The recorded 100,000/100,000 example is matched in form, saved record, closure and source views, with zero difference. A counted 99,000 against expected 100,000 is shortage 1,000 paise consistently; excess uses the opposite sign. Sales totals do not change merely to make drawer reconciliation match.

### AH-FIN-04 — stock audit records UTC rather than IST business day

- **Severity/business impact:** Medium, P1. New movement/source evidence can be attributed to the previous business day. The observed timestamp was 12 October UTC but 13 October IST; businessDate incorrectly recorded 12 October. Incorrect historical valuation totals were not demonstrated.
- **Root cause:** [reconcilePosStockAuditBatch](../../../src/features/pos/data/posRepository.ts#L209) uses timestamp.slice(0,10). Other UTC-slice movement defaults need inventory; their failures are not automatically confirmed by this case.
- **Proposed fix/files/workflows:** Reuse an explicit Asia/Kolkata business-date helper consistently in new audit postings and related fallback dates. When posting is moved server-side, use trusted time there. Preserve intentional receipt/refund effective dates as explicit business inputs rather than replacing all dates with today. Files: posRepository, shared/pos date helpers, stock posting handlers and tests.
- **History/compatibility:** Retain original createdAt and businessDate on historical movements. No automatic backdating/correction; explain mismatches honestly. Stock History ordering/filter contracts remain based on their documented timestamp semantics.
- **Regression:** Inject fixed timestamps immediately before/at/after IST midnight, including 2026-10-12T18:29:59.999Z and 18:30:00.000Z; month/year/FY boundaries; non-IST browser timezone; explicit GRN/refund dates; audit/source/history traceability.
- **Acceptance:** New movements at/after that midnight record 2026-10-13; just before record 2026-10-12. The source and movement agree on the intended business date. Explicit approved effective dates and old records remain unchanged.

### Firebase service and policy prerequisites

The proposed trusted handlers extend the existing Functions backend/asia-south1 pattern, without a new database, scheduled job or dependency upgrade. Production Cloud Functions deployment requires Blaze; local emulation does not. [Firebase's official deployment prerequisite](https://firebase.google.com/docs/functions/get-started) was checked during this review. ROADMAP records Blaze, whereas older setup/QA notes say Spark/no Functions; the live billing plan was not queried. Confirm the current plan and incremental invocation/read/write costs read-only before implementing a new daily-operation dependency. Do not silently change billing configuration. If Spark-only operation is required, propose a separately approved trusted backend alternative; client transactions cannot satisfy server-authority acceptance.

Policy inputs still needing explicit resolution are refund discount allocation/legacy incomplete evidence, expense approval semantics, and drawer attribution of cash expenses/vendor payments. Batch 1 can preserve current discount and stock policy without inventing these later workflow rules.

## Recommended first implementation batch — trusted checkout only

This is the smallest coherent P0 posting boundary: one operation and its UI/Rules adapter, closing AH-SEC-03 and AH-SEC-04. It is not a complete financial migration. AH-SEC-02 can still compromise catalogue/stock through another writer, and AH-SEC-01/AH-FIN-01 remain open until their batches. Do not declare checkout's canonical catalogue reads sufficient to secure the whole catalogue. This first batch alone does not justify a financial-ready release.

1. **Contract checkpoint:** Inventory exact bill/movement/event/state/sequence/cashier/handover consumers and all sale writers.
