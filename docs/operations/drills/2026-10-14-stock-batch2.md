- Final owner quantity fence must be coordinated with trusted whole refund/void posting. Do not deploy an intermediate stock-only lock that breaks restitutions; do not treat retained owner permission as a security pass.
- Old direct SDK GRN/audit/creation writes now reject locally with no partial stock/payable effects. Cached installed Android behavior is still manual; no production upgrade announcement was written. Safe rollback never restores unsafe direct posting.
- Legacy posted receipts without price provenance keep prices unless restoration is provable; UI explains the unavailable restoration. Existing missing/unreadable source fields are not invented. Old saving audit batches without the new manifest remain readable historical evidence; they are not silently reconstructed or completed. Before release, drain old active audit/receipt posting sessions and inspect any interrupted legacy source read-only; further maintenance requires approval.
- Reversing a receipt does not provide a new same-invoice reissue capability. Existing reservation/original-GRN policy remains; no invoice-reference reuse, inactive-product reactivation or historical mapping/import tool was invented.
- Receiving and reversal include V2 financial effects but legitimate payments/corrections/cheques remain browser-owned. Those existing summaries/authority gaps are dependencies, not a claim of app-wide financial hardening. Invoice/cost/stock policies are preserved, including full payable separate from partial stock value and receipt-owned cost invalidation.
- Recovery is scoped to actor/project/tab session storage and refresh; deleted/closed tab storage or another computer is not promised recovery. Use source history when the intent is unavailable. Unconfirmed operations are not silently discarded or retried with a new identity.
- No new broad listener/whole-catalogue download. Product/barcode identity queries are exact and limited; controls/results use point reads. Historical price filters use one server OR query and stable cursor ordering over existing movements, including price-bearing receipt/reversal rows once. Four additive stockMovements indexes cover priceChanged with dated all/barcode/product filters and exact-product undated inspection; unused operation result/manifest count indexing is disabled locally. Emulator acceptance does not establish that production indexes are built.
- Callable/compute/logging/profile/control/quote/alias reads and immutable recovery/review writes add usage; pending owner price evidence also increases existing feed activity. Replays need profile+operation reads and no repost writes. Transaction retries can repeat reads. No measured read savings, rupee/month estimate or no-cost claim. No new service/min-instance reservation or billing change.
- 200 lines were tested atomically at handler level for receipts without imposing a new arbitrary line cap; no claim of unlimited size, maximum real-world receipt or production latency. Request/document/transaction limits still apply. [Firestore quotas](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/quotas) and the [transaction query API](https://docs.cloud.google.com/nodejs/docs/reference/firestore/latest/firestore/transaction) informed the design; identity reservations avoid relying on empty-query results alone for uniqueness. Node20 support/lifecycle review remains a separate release prerequisite; no runtime upgrade.
